Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about shop policies, appointment prep, and daily chair availability.

Do you accept walk ins?

walk ins are welcomed whenever chair availability allows. Booking online in advance guarantees your dedicated time slot.

How should I prepare my hair before arriving for an appointment?

Arrive with clean, product free, and thoroughly dry hair. This ensures clippers and shears pass smoothly for maximum precision during fades and styling.

What is your cancellation and rescheduling policy?

Please provide at least 24 hours notice if you need to cancel or adjust your booking. This courtesy allows open slots to be filled by clients currently on the waitlist.
